About

Certificate in Computational Science & Engineering, on top of a mechanical engineering degree.

I'm a mechanical engineering student who spends most of my time either underneath a rocket test stand or inside a CAD model. Through Texas Rocket Engineering Lab and the Longhorn Rocketry Association, I've worked across the full lifecycle of flight hardware — structural design, simulation, manufacturing, and the test campaigns that prove it all actually holds.

Outside of rockets, I like taking things apart to understand them: a twenty-year-old motorcycle, a 3D printer, a combat robot built to survive being hit. My certificate in Computational Science and Engineering rounds that out with a comfort in Python and MATLAB that I put to work on data from tank ruptures, sensor logs, and simulations.
